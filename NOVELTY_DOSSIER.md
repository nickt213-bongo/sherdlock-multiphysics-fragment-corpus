# Novelty dossier

## Defining object

SherdLock evaluates a single load-bearing conservation state: an unlabeled multi-vessel reconstruction plus locally anchored reversible braces that are instantiated through the submitted reconstruction and replayed under private loads. Assembly is therefore neither an endpoint nor an auxiliary label; it is the coordinate system that determines whether conservation actions physically execute. The evaluator then exhaustively executes every coalition of the activated submitted braces and assigns each brace an exact counterfactual responsibility across that lattice.

## Closest public work

- Structure-from-Sherds and Structure-from-Sherds++ reconstruct unordered mixed pottery fragments but do not evaluate a conservation intervention executed through the submitted assembly.
- RePAIR and GARF benchmark real-world 2D/3D reassembly and pose recovery but stop at reconstruction.
- Heritage FEA and topology-optimized reinforcement studies design supports for known assembled geometry, not jointly inferred mixed fragments under pose and object-label ambiguity.
- Fragment completion benchmarks generate missing geometry but do not evaluate reversible bracing under private counterfactual handling panels.
- Cooperative-game/Shapley methods attribute predictions or generic interventions, but the defining object here is different: the coalition members first come into existence through a participant-supplied, gauge-free fragment reconstruction, and the lowest brace responsibility directly gates conservation quality.

## Material difference

The primary evaluated object crosses the boundary between uncertain reconstruction and conservation action. A brace has no standalone predicted utility: its anchors become meaningful only after participant poses are composed, and private load response is conditional on that realized geometry. The new materially distinct evaluator boundary is the **reconstruction-conditioned counterfactual responsibility lattice**: up to 64 brace coalitions are executed, exact Shapley marginals are computed, and the minimum normalized responsibility penalizes redundant stabilization. Vessel labels and global poses remain nuisance gauges, while local action execution and coalition responsibility are gauge-consistent. No located benchmark jointly scores mixed-object grouping, gauge-free pose synchronization, local-anchor intervention execution, exhaustive coalition responsibility, reversibility cost, and worst-load survival.
