# Novelty dossier

## Defining object

SherdLock evaluates a complete conservation certification procedure: locally anchored reversible brace installation through an uncertain latent assembly, private-load handling, and synthesis of a fault-tolerant withdrawal program. Assembly is not a leaderboard objective; it is a feasibility witness. The evaluator exhaustively executes every coalition of activated braces, assigns exact counterfactual responsibility, and certifies a participant-ordered removal schedule against every single adjacent sequencing fault.

## Closest public work

- Structure-from-Sherds and Structure-from-Sherds++ reconstruct unordered mixed pottery fragments but do not evaluate a conservation intervention executed through the submitted assembly.
- RePAIR and GARF benchmark real-world 2D/3D reassembly and pose recovery but stop at reconstruction.
- Heritage FEA and topology-optimized reinforcement studies design supports for known assembled geometry, not jointly inferred mixed fragments under pose and object-label ambiguity.
- Fragment completion benchmarks generate missing geometry but do not evaluate reversible bracing under private counterfactual handling panels.
- Cooperative-game/Shapley methods attribute predictions or generic interventions, but the defining object here is different: the coalition members first come into existence through a participant-supplied, gauge-free fragment reconstruction, and the lowest brace responsibility directly gates conservation quality.
- Reversible-treatment guidance in cultural-heritage conservation emphasizes retreatability and safe removal, but the located reassembly benchmarks do not require a participant to synthesize an ordered withdrawal program and certify its local fault neighborhood over participant-reconstructed geometry.

## Material difference

The evaluated object is no longer “reassembly plus brace selection.” It is an **adversarial retreatability program over latent geometry**. A brace has no standalone utility: its anchors exist only after participant poses are composed, private response depends on that geometry, and removal safety depends on which other braces remain. The participant must jointly choose a coalition and one of up to 720 nominal withdrawal schedules; the grader then executes the nominal program and every one-adjacent-swap fault. Gain above no treatment is gated by exact Shapley responsibility and worst-neighborhood withdrawal safety, then multiplied by reconstruction feasibility. Because neither geometry nor an unordered brace set receives independent credit, the familiar SfS++/RePAIR reconstruction objective is only a latent constraint, not the scored task. No located benchmark evaluates this zero-credit-on-either-side, program-synthesis conservation certificate.
